Catalog your collection

Search Comic Vine or the Grand Comics Database, or import straight from League of Comic Geeks, BDGest, BubbleBD, or Whakoom — covers and details download automatically.

Track status

Owned, ordered, wishlist, for sale, for trade, or loaned — plus front, back, spine, and dust-jacket flap cover images for every book.

Browse everything, connected

By series, publisher, creator, or character, with tappable aliases, roles, and tags linking everything that's related — or scan a comic's barcode from Search to jump straight to it if you already own it.

Reading Maps

Build and save full reading orders from linked reprints, collected editions, and title continuity across your whole library.

Collection Statistics

Spending by year, month, and publisher, and your most expensive purchases, right from Settings.

Backup & restore

Your whole collection via iCloud, Dropbox, or any Files-based storage — including legacy backups from ComicLand's earliest versions.

About ComicLand

ComicLand is a free, independently developed iOS app for organizing your comic book collection. In continuous development since 2014, and available in English, French, Spanish, and many other languages.
