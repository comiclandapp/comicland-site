Privacy Policy

Effective: August 30, 2026 · Last updated: November 14, 2026
← Back to ComicLand

ComicLand runs on your own devices: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android. There is no ComicLand account, no ComicLand server, and no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code anywhere in the app. Your collection stays on your device unless you export it yourself.

What ComicLand stores on your device

Everything you enter is saved in a database on your device. That includes comics, series, creators, publishers, characters, reading orders, notes, prices and status (owned, wishlist, for sale, for trade, loaned). ComicLand never uploads it to a server run by the developer, and no one but you can see it.

Like any app's data, on iPhone and iPad it is part of your device's own iCloud or computer backup if you have that turned on. On Android, ComicLand keeps its database out of Google's automatic backup and device-to-device transfer. To move a library to a new Android phone, use Back up and Restore in Settings.

Permissions

ComicLand only asks for these when you use the feature that needs them:

  • Camera: to scan a comic's barcode, and to take your own photos of covers, back covers, spines or dust jackets.
  • Photos: to choose cover images from your photo library. On iPhone and iPad, ComicLand also remembers which library photo each cover came from, so it can find the same photo again after you restore a backup. The Mac app reads your Photos library, without changing it, to show those covers.
  • Network (Android lists this separately): to search comic databases and load cover images.

Android's permission list also shows a few background-task permissions (foreground service, run at startup, keep awake). They come from Android's standard background-work library. ComicLand does not use them for anything.

Your photos and scans never leave your device. A search sends only the barcode or the text you typed.

Comic databases you connect

ComicLand can search, import from and refresh your collection with other comic services. Connecting one is optional. When you do, here is exactly what ComicLand keeps:

Service How you sign in What ComicLand keeps
Comic Vine You paste in your own API key from comicvine.gamespot.com Your API key.
League of Comic Geeks Their own sign-in page, inside the app The session their site sets. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, also the username and password you type into ComicLand's form, if you use it. On Android, only a note of your username.
Whakoom Their own sign-in page, inside the app The session their site sets. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, also the username and password you type into ComicLand's form.
BDGest A form inside ComicLand Your email and password.
BDThèque Their own sign-in page, inside the app The session their site sets. No password.
BubbleBD A form inside ComicLand The sign-in token BubbleBD issues. Your password is used once to sign in and is not kept.
Grand Comics Database A form inside ComicLand (optional; it raises your daily lookup limit) Your username and password. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, also the session from connecting your GCD collection, if you do.
Tebeosfera A form inside ComicLand Your email or username and password, and the session their site sets.
Google Books (iPhone, iPad and Mac) Optionally, you paste in your own API key Your API key, if you add one.

Passwords, keys and tokens are kept in secure storage on your device. On iPhone, iPad and Mac that's the Keychain, set to this device only, so they never sync to iCloud Keychain. On Android, they're encrypted with a key held by the Android Keystore. Each one is sent only to its own service's servers, to sign you in there. They are never sent to the developer or to any analytics or advertising company.

ComicLand also looks up comics in Open Library, which needs no sign-in. Cover images load directly from wherever each service hosts them. That includes Amazon S3 for League of Comic Geeks.

Backup and export

ComicLand can export your collection as a file, saved wherever you pick with your device's file picker. The developer never receives it.

  • iPhone and iPad: the export holds your collection data. Cover images you added are left out unless you tick "Include images". The export also holds the photo-library references described above.
  • Android: the export holds your collection database only. Cover images are not included.

Other things that leave your device, only when you choose

  • Sharing: sharing a cover or image hands that one image to the app you pick.
  • Technical support: this opens your own mail app with the app version in the subject. Nothing is sent until you send it.
  • Apple Watch: the watch app gets series lists and details from your iPhone, nowhere else.
  • Home Screen widget (iPhone and iPad): it shows your recent series from your library on the device, and loads their covers directly from where those images are hosted.
  • Mac: the Mac app opens a ComicLand library file that you choose.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

ComicLand has no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code of any kind. On Android it doesn't use Google Play Services either. Nothing about how you use the app is collected or sent to the developer or to anyone else for tracking or advertising.

Children's privacy

ComicLand is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. It has no account system and keeps data on your device, so it collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

To disconnect one service, delete its sign-in in ComicLand's settings (on most services this is Sign Out). That removes everything ComicLand kept for that service. Uninstalling ComicLand removes your collection database.

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, the system can keep Keychain items after you delete an app. Delete each service's sign-in before uninstalling if you want those removed too. On Android, uninstalling removes everything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes too. If ComicLand ever adds an online account or sync, this policy will be updated to describe it before that ships. Continued use of ComicLand after an update means you accept the revised policy.
